About me

Professional organizer in Herning, trained Simple Life Organizer.

Carina Birch · Professional organizer

Hi, I'm Carina

I have always loved putting things into systems, whether it was my own childhood bedroom or deadlines and milestones in projects at work.

I graduated with a master's in Educational Science from Aarhus University in 2020 and have since worked as a project coordinator and development consultant. In both jobs an eye for detail and structure was essential.

In 2023 I started the Simple Life Organizer training with Marianne Gammelgaard, owner of Simple Life. During the training I did placements around Denmark and gained experience organising every kind of room.
