This week’s trends, methods and habits from the world of organising, with links to the sources.

Week 42 · 2026

BeforeAfter

Habit

Micro-tidying: a little every day

Instead of one big clear-out day, small fixed habits win, like ten minutes in the evening putting things back. It cuts decision fatigue and keeps the piles away.

Read the source: AOL / Real Simple ↗
BeforeAfter

Trend

Storage that decorates

Storage is no longer hidden away. Glass, bamboo, wood and woven baskets make the shelf part of the interior, and plastic is on its way out.

Read the source: Sourcy ↗
BeforeAfter

Method

Fewer, better systems

More boxes rarely fix the mess. Clutter usually comes from postponed decisions. Choose what stays first, then build a few simple systems that fit everyday life.

Read the source: Livingetc ↗
BeforeAfter

Trend

Low stimulation living

Danish trend researcher Louise Byg Kongsholm sees a wish for fewer impressions and more calm in everyday life. A home with fewer things on display is a good place to start.

Read the source: TV 2 Kosmopol ↗
