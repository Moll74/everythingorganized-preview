Prices

Hourly rates for homes and businesses. We agree the hours beforehand, so you know the price.

Before every job we agree together on how many hours it will take. If it needs more than 7 hours, it is split over two consecutive days.

Private homes

  • DKK 625per hour incl. VAT
  • Discount for a full day of organising.
  • Travel is charged by agreement.

Businesses

Choose between organising done by me alone, or a shared approach where selected staff take part, so they can run and maintain the systems afterwards.

  • DKK 500per hour excl. VAT, done by the organizer
  • DKK 700per hour excl. VAT, shared approach focused on maintenance
  • Travel is charged at the Danish state rates.

Before I arrive

Every job is individual, and your wishes come first. Before I visit, we agree on:

  1. The time frame of the job, and so the price
  2. Your role in the four steps
  3. Storage options
  4. Payment

Questions and answers

What does it cost?

For private homes DKK 625 per hour incl. VAT, with a discount for a full day. Businesses pay DKK 500 per hour excl. VAT, or DKK 700 per hour excl. VAT for a shared approach with staff.

How long does it take?

We agree on that beforehand, based on the job. If it needs more than 7 hours, it is split over two consecutive days.

Do I need to take part?

Yes, you help sort. You decide what goes on to be reused, sold or taken to the tip. We agree on your role in the four steps beforehand.
