Questions and answers

What people ask most before they book.

Price and booking

What does it cost?

For private homes DKK 625 per hour incl. VAT, with a discount for a full day. Businesses pay DKK 500 per hour excl. VAT, or DKK 700 per hour excl. VAT for a shared approach with staff.

Do you give a discount?

Yes, private customers get a discount for a full day of organising. We agree on it when we plan the job.

How do I pay?

Payment is one of the points we agree on before I visit.

How do I book?

Call +45 61 60 72 74, write to kontakt@everythingorganized.dk or use the form on the site. Tell me a little about the room or the job, and we agree on time and price in a no-obligation chat.

What about travel?

For private customers travel is charged by agreement. For businesses at the Danish state rates.

Private homes

Which rooms can you help with?

Every room. I have experience with kitchens, wardrobes, craft rooms, bookshelves and workshops.

Do I need to take part?

Yes, you help sort. You decide what goes on to be reused, sold or taken to the tip. We agree on your role in the four steps beforehand.

How does a visit work?

Before I come, we agree on the time frame and so the price, your role, the storage options and payment. On the day we work through the four steps of the Simple Life Method: arrange, categorise, sort and organise.

How long does it take?

We agree on that beforehand, based on the job. If it needs more than 7 hours, it is split over two consecutive days.

Should I buy boxes and storage beforehand?

We agree on that before I come. Storage options are one of the fixed points we talk about before the visit.

What happens to the things I sort out?

You decide. While sorting, you choose what goes on to be reused, what gets sold and what goes to the tip. Everything else gets a fixed place in the new system.

Businesses

Do you work with businesses?

Yes. You can choose to have me organise alone, or a shared approach where selected staff take part, so they can maintain the systems themselves.

What is the difference between homes and businesses?

For private homes it is DKK 625 per hour incl. VAT, and you take part in the sorting. Businesses choose between two models: I organise on my own at DKK 500 per hour excl. VAT, or a shared approach at DKK 700, where selected staff take part.

What is the shared approach for businesses?

Selected staff take part in the process, so they learn the system and can run and maintain it themselves afterwards. It costs DKK 700 per hour excl. VAT. Organising done by me alone costs DKK 500.

About me and the method

What is your background?

I am a trained Simple Life Organizer with Simple Life and hold an MA in Educational Science from Aarhus University. Before becoming an organizer I worked as a project coordinator and development consultant, where structure and an eye for detail were essential.

Were you on «Vi drukner i rod»?

Yes, I worked behind the camera on season 3 of the Danish TV 2 series «Vi drukner i rod» (We're drowning in clutter).

What is the Simple Life Method?

A method in four steps: arrange, categorise, sort and organise. It makes sure the system in the room is built for the things that should actually stay there, so it is easy to keep.

Can I see examples of your work?

Yes. Under «From clutter to system» you can see rooms I have organised, and under Inspiration you will find my most-watched TikTok videos. There is more on Instagram, TikTok and Facebook.

Where do you work?

In Herning and the surrounding area. For private homes travel is charged by agreement, for businesses at the Danish state rates.
