The Simple Life Method

Four steps from clutter to calm

I work with the Simple Life Method. The four steps make sure the system in a room is built around the things that actually have a right to be there.

ReuseSellTip
1Empty2Categorise3Sort4Organise
  1. Step 1 of 4

    Empty

    Everything comes out of the room, so we start with a blank canvas.

  2. Step 2 of 4

    Categorise

    Everything is sorted into categories, so it becomes clear what you have a lot of.

  3. Step 3 of 4

    Sort

    Sorting is what makes everything fit back into the same room. You decide what goes on to be reused, sold or taken to the tip.

  4. Step 4 of 4

    Organise

    Everything goes back into the room and gets a permanent place in a logical system, where things of the same kind live together.
